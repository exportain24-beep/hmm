**Description / caption**

```
How to rig a pilot ladder the way the new IMO pilot transfer standards require: 1.5 to 9 metres climb, position clear of discharges and flat on the hull, 48 kN strong points and the gear at the access point. From 1 January 2028 ladders also leave service after 30 months in use or 36 months from manufacture.

In this video:
• Pilot ladder alone: 1.5 to 9 m
• Clear, inside the parallel body, flat on the hull
• Strong points, not shortcuts
• Ready before the pilot boat arrives
• Ladder withdrawn from service after
• Inspect before and after every use

Who should watch: OOW, Deck Cadet, Bosun, Chief Officer.
```
