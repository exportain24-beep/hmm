Enclosed spaces on board can hold an atmosphere that cannot support life, with no warning to the senses. The precautions every seafarer must follow before entry: permit, isolation, ventilation, testing, attendant and rescue readiness.

In this video:
• You cannot see or smell low oxygen
• Oxygen by volume before entry
• Enclosed space entry permit issued
• Space isolated and ventilated
• Atmosphere tested, then monitored
• Attendant at the entrance

Who should watch: Rating, Deck Cadet, Engine Cadet, Junior Engineer, OOW.
