6. SHORT-FORM CONTENT REQUIREMENT
========================================================

Every Short must teach ONE complete concept.

Target duration:

50–60 seconds.

Preferred duration:

approximately 55 seconds.

The Short must NOT feel like a list of facts.

It must feel like a mini maritime lesson.

Structure:

HOOK
→ CONTEXT
→ EXPLANATION
→ PRACTICAL EXAMPLE
→ COMMON MISTAKE
→ TAKEAWAY

Recommended timing:

0–5 sec:
Strong hook.

5–12 sec:
Introduce the situation.

12–38 sec:
Explain the concept clearly.

38–50 sec:
Realistic shipboard example.

50–56 sec:
Common mistake or important warning.

56–60 sec:
One memorable takeaway.

Do not cram multiple unrelated concepts into one Short.

One Short = One concept.
